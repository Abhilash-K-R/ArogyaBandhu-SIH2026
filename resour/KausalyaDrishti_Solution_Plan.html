KausalyaDrishti (ಕೌಶಲ್ಯ ದೃಷ್ಟಿ)

AI-Based Real-Time Monitoring of Training Centres for Attendance and Infrastructure Compliance
📌 Problem Statement in One Line
Ministry of Skill Development (PS 26245): Government skilling schemes (PMKVY, DDU-GKY) suffer from attendance falsification (ghost trainees claimed for government stipends) and non-compliant lab infrastructure between manual periodic audits.
💡 Our Solution in One Line
KausalyaDrishti is an edge-AI video compliance system that processes training centre camera feeds in real time to count actual students, detect mandatory machinery, blur faces for 100% trainee privacy, and flag discrepancies to an MSDE ministry command dashboard using only 5 KB of rural bandwidth.

🎯 Core Deliverables & PS Requirements Mapping

Official Requirement KausalyaDrishti Implementation
Real-Time Attendance Estimation Client-side AI headcount detector continuously counting trainees in classroom video feeds.
Cross-Checking with Portal Records Tally engine comparing centre's portal claims vs AI headcount ➔ Flags Ghost Trainee Fraud.
Infrastructure Presence & Operability Computer vision object detector scanning for sanctioned workbenches, sewing machines, welding kits.
Central Discrepancy Dashboard MSDE Ministry dashboard with district heatmaps, center compliance ratings, and grant freeze actions.
Trainee Privacy Preservation Automatic dynamic face pixelation/blurring filter conforming to DPDP Act standards.
Low-Bandwidth Rural Mode Transmits 5 KB JSON metadata telemetry + violation keyframe snapshots instead of 24/7 video.

🏗️ 3-Portal System Architecture

🎥 Centre Edge Camera Portal
Runs at Training Centre: Processes webcam/CCTV feed with live bounding box overlays and privacy face-blur.
📊 Audit & Discrepancy Desk
Automated Audit: Tally comparison between registered roster and live AI count + Machinery checklist.
🏛️ MSDE Ministry Dashboard
Used by MSDE / NSDC Officers: National/State risk heatmaps, show-cause notices, grant freezes.
