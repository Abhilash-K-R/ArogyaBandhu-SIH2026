ArogyaBandhu (ಆರೋಗ್ಯ ಬಂಧು)

Complete Technical & Operational Architecture Plan | 100% Offline-First AI Tele-Triage Platform
📌 Problem Statement in One Line
Government of Maharashtra (PS 133): Rural communities lack accessible, quality public healthcare due to travel barriers, offline connectivity blackouts, language gaps, fragmented records, and delayed referrals.
💡 Our Solution in One Line
ArogyaBandhu is an offline-first, bilingual (Kannada + English), AI-triage platform that empowers frontline ASHA workers to detect high-risk maternal, child, and chronic cases at the doorstep, generate instant QR referral slips, and seamlessly connect with PHC medical officers without requiring internet connectivity.

🙋 Anticipated Judges' Questions & High-Impact Answers

Q1: What makes this different from existing portals like RCH or Poshan Tracker?
Existing government portals are retrospective compliance reporting tools (data entered days later for administrative records). ArogyaBandhu is a point-of-care clinical decision support system that guides the health worker during the physical encounter, triages risk on the spot, and operates 100% offline.
Q2: How does the app guarantee zero data loss during prolonged offline periods?
We utilize browser IndexedDB transactional persistence with a queued synchronization state machine. Encrypted payload chunks remain safely stored on the device across browser restarts and power cycles until an HTTP 200 OK acknowledgment is received from the PHC gateway.
Q3: How is patient privacy maintained in accordance with national health standards?
We implement ABDM (Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission) compliance using 14-digit ABHA tokens. Data at rest is encrypted, and strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) ensures ASHAs only access assigned village households, doctors view facility referrals, and district officers receive de-identified epidemiological aggregations.
Q4: How does this strengthen rather than replace the existing public health apparatus?
ArogyaBandhu acts as a digital nervous system strictly respecting the National Health Mission (NHM) tiered hierarchy: Sub-Centre ASHA → PHC Medical Officer → Taluk/District Hospital → 108 Arogya Kavacha. It removes friction without requiring any bureaucratic restructuring.
